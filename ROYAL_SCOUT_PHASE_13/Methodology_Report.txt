
ROYAL SCOUT INTELLIGENCE 2027
PRESSURE PERFORMANCE SCOUTING METHODOLOGY

DATASET
Selected IPL and T20I competitions from 2022 onward.
837 selected matches.
193,988 selected ball-by-ball records.

PRESSURE MODEL
Pressure is calculated using pre-delivery match state.
First innings uses match stage and wickets remaining.
Second innings additionally uses required run rate.

Pressure categories:
Low: below 35
Medium: 35 to below 65
High: 65 and above

Normal performance = Low pressure.
Pressure performance = Medium + High pressure.

BATTER SCORING
Pressure Strike Rate: 40%
Strike Rate Improvement: 25%
Pressure Average: 20%
Sample Reliability: 15%

BOWLER SCORING
Pressure Wicket Rate: 60%
Pressure Legal Balls: 25%
Pressure Matches: 15%

ELIGIBILITY
Batters: At least 60 pressure balls and 10 pressure matches.
Bowlers: At least 120 pressure legal balls and 10 pressure matches.

LIMITATIONS
1. Default scheduled innings length is 120 balls where revised overs are unavailable.
2. Some D/L matches lack reliable revised targets.
3. Bowling economy remains provisional due to extras reconciliation.
4. IPL and T20I performances are combined.
5. Pressure thresholds and scoring weights are project-defined heuristics.
6. Results should be interpreted within these data limitations.
